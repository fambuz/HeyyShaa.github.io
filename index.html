something I wanted to say
Dear you,

I spent way too long trying to figure out what to write here. Then I realised that I didn't need some perfect, dramatic speech. I just needed to be honest.

I really like you. I like the way you can make me laugh, the little things you do without thinking about them, and the memories that somehow become my favourite parts of the day.

I don't know exactly where life is going to take us, and I'm not writing this expecting you to give me some huge answer. I just wanted you to know that you are genuinely important to me.

So this is my slightly embarrassing, slightly cheesy, completely handmade way of saying: I'm really glad I met you.
